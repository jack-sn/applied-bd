Driving High Engagement - The Alignment Race

Comprehensive Mobilization of Your Teams and Alignment with the Business Goals

Target
  • The Engagement Race is a very innovative and effective workshop format to vitalizes, mobilizes and realigns your company.
  • It enables you to firmly establish your strategic intent in your organization. As well, together with your teams you can solve acute problems in a highly efficient and time saving manner: e. g.

"how can we significantly improve our sales processes and win more deals?" or "how can we smoothly communicate the planned price increase?"

  • The Engagement Race is scalable and can also be applied to multiple and international teams. This critical mass generates a sustainable and dynamic process for the target driven development of your teams and organisation.
Content
  • The Engagement Race is set up like a Formula 1 Race with challenging "Pit Stops", which are designed specifically to solve your major business problems.
  • In these „Pit Stops", the participants face up to current or future business challenges driven by changing customer needs or aggressive competitors.
  • The topics are always adapted to your individual business, needs and targets.
Method
  • The Engagement Race runs at high speed. It is competitive and very interactive.
  • It creates a supportive learning atmosphere and it facilitates the problem solving process.
  • The participants work under a positive tension, while intensively discussing winning business ideas or removing costly process barriers.
  • Typically, a Race consists of 4 to 12 teams with 5 members each.
  • The mix is cross-functional and cross-locational.
  • It can be organized simultaneously at multiple international locations.

Preparation Interviews of representatives from different functions and hierarchy levels to adapt the „Pit Stops" to your business needs.

Sustainability

The Engagement Race finally results in working groups, which will drive the implementation of the Race outcome - supported by the management.

Target Group
  • The Engagement Race is targeted at all employees who are required to solve the selected business problems.
  • The management is actively involved in the Race and will benefit from the intense dialogue with their staff about individual perceptions, business challenges and promising solutions.
Duration

One day.

Prior, the participants will be tuned to the Race by a targeted online questionnaire.
