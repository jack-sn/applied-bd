Increasing Customer Impact

Driving sales performance in customer contact situations

Objective

Increasing Customer Impact Workshops are specifically designed to improve the ability to present offerings, customer benefits and the other issues relevant to a sales process so persuasively that they favorably impress the customer and are thoroughly understood.

Benefits
  • Participants are highly motivated to better reach customers
  • Differentiating beside technology through high impact interaction skills
  • Transforming technical benefits into a customer specific value proposition
  • Live learning with strong feedback on personal behavior
  • Aligning company needs with customer needs
Content
  • Ad-hoc presentation
  • Understanding human learning and perception
  • Managing the audience
  • Defining the target and core messages in line with your own positioning
  • Setting up a winning life presentation that fits your own culture
  • Making best use of different modes of communication
  • Presentation tools to maximize impact on customer (e.g. media mix)
  • Having a ready tongue and coping with killer phrases
  • Securing next steps and contracting
Preparation
  • Upfront preparation is needed
  • The participants will prepare a real customer presentation for the workshop.

  • Video feedback, ad-hoc presentation, company specific life presentations
  • Beyond PowerPoint!
Sustainability
  • Training (2 days; optional virtual format)
  • Home work, follow-up session, individual coaching on request
Target Audience

The workshop is targeted at all sales professionals and sales related people, including account managers, sales managers, business development, marketing, service

Duration
  • Two days (optional virtual format)
  • An optional implementation review takes place three months after the workshop
