Business Matching at International Tradeshows

Maximizing your exhibitor benefits through targeted leads generation

Objectives
  • To help you as an exhibitor getting the maximum exposure at the tradeshow
  • To reach out to your best potential clients or sales partners
  • To get a comprehensive market overview in your discussions with industry experts
  • To open-up new market opportunities and build up relationship with European companies
Benefits
  • Lead generation: increase booth traffic via pre-show marketing (e.g. targeted invitation mailings) and pre-arranged meetings with potential clients and business partners
  • Sales driven meeting efficiency: provide on-site service to assist you with professional sales and marketing know-how and to facilitate business meetings at the tradeshow
  • Sustainable business development: follow-up with your leads qualified at the tradeshow
Pre-show
  • Develop a long list of target clients from professional databases fitting your business activities
  • Prepare invitation letters and marketing flyers for targeted mailings
  • Prepare website and other marketing material to fit your exhibitor targets
  • Contact top target companies to set up business appointments at the tradeshow
  • Provide you with meeting schedule and matching companies' details for preparation
Activities
  • Train exhibitor staff for efficient lead selection and briefing on potential questions at the show
  • Act as facilitator at each matching session
  • Meet you at the end of the program for business conclusions and feedback
Post-show
  • Process and organize leads information gathered at the show activities
  • Provide written post-show summary, analysis and action plan
  • Follow-up on business opportunities
Experiences

International tradeshows in (examples)

  • Munich (e.g. electronica, Productronica, IFAT, Laser, Analytica, Bauma)
  • Frankfurt (e.g. Achema, IAA)
  • Hannover (Industrial Fair, CeBIT)
  • Düsseldorf (K-Messe)
  • London (Farnborough Airshow)
  • Barcelona (Mobile World Congress)
Duration
  • Each business matching session last 45 minutes
  • Number of sessions upon request (e.g. 8 sessions in the course of 2 days)
