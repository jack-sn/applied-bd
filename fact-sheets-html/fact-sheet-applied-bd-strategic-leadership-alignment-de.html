Strategic Leadership Alignment - Workshop

High performance Management Team creating a unified company and driving sales excellence

Objectives
& Benefits

Empowerment of your teams in engineering, sales or elsewhere needs to be combined with direction and engagement:

  • Creating a strong and shared corporate / group / team Vision : how does “great” look like
  • Understanding the Purpose of the Leadership Team: contribution and focus in making the vision become true
  • Defining Strategy and “ Must-Win-Battles ”: plan and focus on What needs to get done
  • Agreeing on Values and Leadership Principles : How we get it done
  • Setting up an Action Plan : next steps and control points

This common understanding of the Leadership Team establishes the basis for further mobilization campaigns, especially in global sales and marketing to drive revenue

Pre-Workshop

Activities

  • Clearly communicated commitment and endorsement of the planned Leadership Workshop by the responsible executive member
  • Interviews of selected leaders of the company to support the necessary buy-in and to calibrate the content of the Leadership Workshop
  • Survey among significant representatives of the company’s functions, businesses, hierarchy levels and regions (in English and local language)
  • Final alignment of the Leadership Workshop concept with the One-Company core team

Post-Workshop

Activities

  • Documentation and summary of the results
  • Presentation of the results to the Leadership Team
