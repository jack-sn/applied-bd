Senior Leaders and Executive Coaching

Increasing your impact, reaching your targets better

Focus

The dialog with the coach is targeted at increasing your professional impact by developing solutions for current and future business challenges.

These are examples of some typical results of a coaching:

  • Getting things done: better performance, KPI achievement and work efficiency
  • Frame setting: strategy development and company restructuring
  • Leading challenging teams and individuals more efficiently
  • Energy management: mobilization of workforce and alignment with a common goal
  • Improved impact in interactions (e. g. presentations) with customers, shareholders and staff
  • Work-life-balance and mental / physical stability
  • Optimum preparation for new assignments or outplacement

Content and
Benefit

The agenda of the coaching is elaborated and agreed upon with you individually.
We recommend conducting a management potential assessment or 360-feedback survey to get a clearer picture of your strengths and weaknesses.

Some examples of topics that are addressed in a coaching session are:

  • Clarify your role, challenges, aspiration and targets (work & life)
  • Identify recurring patterns, barriers and supportive elements towards these targets
  • Develop and apply measures to remove these barriers
  • Improve your efficiency through clear priority setting, time management and by closing loops
  • Know your own strength and weaknesses and learn how to balance these better
  • Understand your impression on others and drive it consciously
  • Analyze challenges and opportunities in your interaction with key people in your business
  • Actively manage these stake holders and their expectations (customers, management, staff, etc.)
  • Efficiently lead your teams in a high performance environment (incl. management of regionally distributed workforce)
  • Nurture the (high potential) talents in your teams
  • Manage your own development and career

Target Group

Senior managers and executives who want to reach their targets more successfully and more efficiently and who want to be better prepared for future challenges.

Process and
Duration

Initial “chem-check” session; prep-work incl. collecting and evaluating feedback from stakeholders; two half-day off-site sessions for baselining, target setting and action plan; weekly 60 min. coaching sessions over a period of three months to facilitate the implementation of the action plan (online or face-to-face); final review incl. manager after 3 months.

Thereafter, additional 3 months of on demand coaching calls (with 15 min. slot per week blocked in calendar)
