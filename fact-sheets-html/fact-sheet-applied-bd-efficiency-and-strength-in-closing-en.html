Efficiency and Strength in Closing

Organizational and personal efficiency for increased customer impact and sales

Target

“Efficiency and Strength in Closing” is focusing on the participant’s capability to increase personal efficiency by clearly defining individual goals, managing internal and external stakeholders and increasing internal sales efficiency to successfully close a deal.

In particular, the participants optimize their own sales process by identifying and removing barriers. This creates faster and more efficient sales cycles, improves the hit-rate and leads to higher revenues.

Benefit

  • “Cleaned house” to increase personal efficiency
  • Removal of barriers for personal and organizational sales excellence
  • Increased momentum and actively managed internal sales efficiency for increased strength in closing
  • Sustaining implementation through detailed action plan for the coming 90 days

Content

  • Sales process analysis
  • Actively managing internal sales efficiency
  • Barriers for personal and organizational sales performance
  • Removing barriers
  • Increasing personal efficiency
  • Managing personal time and work load
  • Individual contribution to organizational performance
  • Project qualification profile to focus on the right “battles”
  • Efficient sales reviews for better resource utilization
  • Setting priorities right and preparing for efficient customer meetings
  • Clear ownership, goal setting and follow-through to get the deal
  • Action planning, commitment and management support to get things done

Preparation

  • Activating the participants before the workshop
  • Selecting real sales projects for the workshop

Methodology

Highly interactive team coaching (8 – 12 participants)

Duration and
Sustainability

  • Two days’ workshop plus preparation (optional virtual format)
  • An optional implementation review takes place three months after the workshop.
